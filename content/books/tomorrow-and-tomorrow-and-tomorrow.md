On a bitter-cold day, in the December of his junior year at Harvard, Sam Masur exits a subway car and sees, amid the hordes of people waiting on the platform, Sadie Green. He calls her name. For a moment, she pretends she hasn't heard him. Then, she turns. At the center of this unforgettable story is the creative partnership between Sam and Sadie—two friends who meet as children and reunite as adults to create video games, finding an intimacy in digital worlds that eludes them in their real lives.
