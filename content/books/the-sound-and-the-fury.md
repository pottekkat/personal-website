_**The Sound and the Fury**_ is where I'm going after [As I Lay Dying](/books/as-i-lay-dying/). I came to Faulkner through the 2023 film _Perfect Days_, and then [this TED-Ed video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wYQWh8VGL3M) gave me an idea of what I'm getting into.

Three Compson brothers narrate clashing versions of the same story, slipping between past and present without warning. Faulkner wants you to share in their confusion.

{{< blockquote author="TED-Ed" link="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wYQWh8VGL3M" title="How (and why) to read William Faulkner" >}}
Reading Faulkner is rarely easy, but it is deeply rewarding. He invites readers to contemplate the unreliable nature of history and memory.
