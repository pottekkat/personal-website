_**The Scent of Time: A Philosophical Essay on the Art of Lingering**_ is a book Catherine K. has a love-hate relationship with. In ["Fall thoughts, fall reads"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJ_Z4rOnjak), she says it changed how she perceives time: we live it too fast, and the anticipation before something happens is part of the experience.
