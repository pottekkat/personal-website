_**The Post-Office Girl**_ is a book Catherine K. was ready to hate, as she says in ["I read to taste life twice"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JLwsWW9aBpc). Zweig won her over with Christine, a poor post office clerk who gets a taste of luxury that she can never go back to.

{{< blockquote author="Catherine K." link="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JLwsWW9aBpc" title="I read to taste life twice" >}}
If I were an alien sent to Earth and I wanted to figure out the workings of human minds, I would read this book.
{{< /blockquote >}}
