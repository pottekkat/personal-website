_**As I Lay Dying**_ is one of the two Faulkner novels on my list, along with [The Sound and the Fury](/books/the-sound-and-the-fury/). I came to Faulkner through the 2023 film _Perfect Days_, where Hirayama reads _The Wild Palms_ before falling asleep.

This one is told by fifteen different narrators as a family carries their mother's coffin across Mississippi to bury her. It is supposedly the more approachable of the two, so I plan to start here.
