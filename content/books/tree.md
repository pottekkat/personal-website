_**Tree**_ is a collection of essays about trees, another book I got from _Perfect Days_ after [Eleven](/books/eleven/). Hirayama spends his lunch breaks photographing the light falling through the leaves above him, which the Japanese call _komorebi_, so it makes sense that he would be reading this.

The English translation came out only recently, decades after Aya Kōda wrote these essays, which might be the film's doing.
