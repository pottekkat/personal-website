_**What Happened in History**_ showed up in a [video about "Bookland"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=68vS8CTBL3k), the fictional country that all ISBN barcodes come from. The video ends with an old Pelican paperback that has no barcode and no ISBN, just a Standard Book Number from the 1960s. That paperback is this book.

Interestingly, this book seems to be recommended a lot by Marxists, and V. Gordon Childe was a socialist himself.
