_**The Passion According to G.H.**_ is widely considered Clarice Lispector's magnum opus. Apparently, a woman walks into her maid's empty room, sees a cockroach, and the rest of the book happens inside her head.

It is one of several Lispector books on my list, along with [Near to the Wild Heart](/books/near-to-the-wild-heart/), [Água Viva](/books/agua-viva/), and [A Breath of Life](/books/a-breath-of-life/).
