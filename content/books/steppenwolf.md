_**Steppenwolf**_ is about a man torn between society and his need to reject it. Zoë Winter calls it one of her favorite novels in [her video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CDbXzVwdyo8) on [The Age of Reason](/books/the-age-of-reason/), and says the two have a similar mocking yet comforting tone. The difference, she says, is that Steppenwolf ends on a note of hope.

I have two other Hesse books on my list, [The Glass Bead Game](/books/the-glass-bead-game/) and [Siddhartha](/books/siddhartha/), but I want to start here.
