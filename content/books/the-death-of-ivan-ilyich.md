_**The Death of Ivan Ilyich**_ is the only book by Leo Tolstoy I will read in full. I only read the abridged version of _War and Peace_ when I was younger, which, I guess, defeats the point. This is a novella, just above a hundred pages, and I thought it would be a good introduction to his work and might lead to reading his bigger novels.
