_**Astrophysics for People in a Hurry**_ is a book I've had for a while but could never get to. From my brief encounters with astrophysics during high school and university, I can confidently say I'm only meant to be a pop culture astrophysics enthusiast. Hopefully, this provides me with enough context to understand the breakthroughs in the field.
