_**Água Viva**_ (also published as _The Stream of Life_) is another Clarice Lispector, this one from Catherine K.'s ["Fall thoughts, fall reads"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJ_Z4rOnjak). She compares reading it to standing in front of a painting in a gallery and finding your own meaning in it.

{{< blockquote author="Catherine K." link="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJ_Z4rOnjak" title="Fall thoughts, fall reads" >}}
Her writing is so ambiguous but so bullseye target into a lot of human experiences.
