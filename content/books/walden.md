_**Walden**_ is Henry David Thoreau's account of the two years he lived in a cabin near Walden Pond. I have wanted to read Thoreau for a while, and Catherine K.'s ["Fall thoughts, fall reads"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJ_Z4rOnjak) gave me a nudge. She ends her section on it with the book's final lines:

{{< blockquote author="Henry David Thoreau" link="" title="Walden" >}}
Only that day dawns to which we are awake. There is more day to dawn. The sun is but a morning star.
{{< /blockquote >}}
