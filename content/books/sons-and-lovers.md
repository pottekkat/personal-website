_**Sons and Lovers**_ is where I'm starting with D. H. Lawrence. I have [The Rainbow](/books/the-rainbow/) and [Women in Love](/books/women-in-love/) on my list too, but this is his earliest and the most autobiographical of the three, drawn from his childhood in a mining town in Nottinghamshire.
