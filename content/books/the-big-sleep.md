_**The Big Sleep**_ is a detective novel by Raymond Chandler, whose writing came up in the [same video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AlF3fWHIu_I) that led me to [The Fixer](/books/the-fixer/). The video quotes a Chandler line as an example of writing built on close observation.

This is Chandler's first novel and the first with Philip Marlowe. [The Maltese Falcon](/books/the-maltese-falcon/) is the other detective novel on my list.
