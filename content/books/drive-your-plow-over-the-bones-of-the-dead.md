_**Drive Your Plow Over the Bones of the Dead**_ hooked me with the title. I've wanted to read this since I first came across it years back, when Olga Tokarczuk received the Nobel prize in literature, but I couldn't find a paperback edition, I think. I came across one recently and bought it. I did not expect it to be a mystery-thriller (tbh, I did not really expect anything), but I'm pleasantly surprised.
