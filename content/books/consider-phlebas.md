_**Consider Phlebas**_ was the most recommended starting point for getting into space science fiction. The premise is compelling, and I can see how the world could become richer in the later books in the _Culture_ series. But it's too dull, and the non-boring parts are too gory. I plan to read the next book in the series only because I'm interested in this world, but if the story does not pick up, I would probably drop it.
