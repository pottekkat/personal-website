_**Letters to a Young Poet**_ is a collection of letters Rilke wrote to a young cadet who had sent him his poems for critique. I got it, along with four other books, from Catherine K.'s ["Fall thoughts, fall reads"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJ_Z4rOnjak), where she says the letters, written in 1903, still feel fresh and that they humble her:

{{< blockquote author="Catherine K." link="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJ_Z4rOnjak" title="Fall thoughts, fall reads" >}}
Where a great and unique person speaks, the rest of us should be silent, and I thought that was just very humbling.
{{< /blockquote >}}

The other four are [The Scent of Time](/books/the-scent-of-time-a-philosophical-essay-on-the-art-of-lingering/), [Água Viva](/books/agua-viva/), [Walden](/books/walden/), and [Swann's Way](/books/swanns-way/).
