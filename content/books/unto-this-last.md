_**Unto This Last**_ is the John Ruskin essay that changed Gandhi's life. In [episode 10 of Watching the Wheels](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LqoXRrhmAiw), the hosts tell the story: Henry Polak gave Gandhi the book before a train journey in South Africa, he read it overnight, and decided to change his life completely. He later translated it into Gujarati as _Sarvodaya_.

I want to read John Ruskin because of how much he influenced Gandhi.
