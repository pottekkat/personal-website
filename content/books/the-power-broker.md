_**The Power Broker**_ is Robert Caro's biography of Robert Moses, the man who built most of modern New York without ever being elected to anything. I found it through Dwarkesh Patel's [video essay on Caro's books](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kX_WmQK_tt0), which he made while preparing to interview Caro. The essay also covers Caro's books on Lyndon Johnson and how he turned the Senate majority leader into a job with actual power.
