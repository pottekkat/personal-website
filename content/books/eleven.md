_**Eleven**_ is a short story collection I found through the 2023 film _Perfect Days_. Hirayama cleans public toilets in Tokyo and reads a paperback every night before he sleeps. He buys this one from a used bookstore midway through the film.

It is one of the books from the film on my list, along with [Tree](/books/tree/) and two by [William Faulkner](/books/the-sound-and-the-fury/).
