_**Franny and Zooey**_ came from Catherine K.'s video ["life is for reading."](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1SS7xrLDV-o), the same one that gave me Jung's [Dreams](/books/dreams/). She says Franny has an existential crisis, her brother Zooey tries to help her, and his answer is that you have to be kind.

{{< blockquote author="Catherine K." link="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1SS7xrLDV-o" title="life is for reading." >}}
True genius level is when you can write something so simple with so much meaning.
{{< /blockquote >}}

Like Dreams, I picked this up as part of my reading on existentialism.
