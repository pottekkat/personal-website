_**The Unbearable Lightness of Being**_ was recommended by ChatGPT after some "discussions" around existentialism and meaning. Although I prefer recommendations from other humans, my quest to find the next book was lazily cut short by an easy prompt. If actual humans are reading this, this is your sign to recommend books to me (please?).

I have this cultivated notion of perfection that I always try to find in art, including paintings, photographs, books, and films. I feel a sense of mutual respect while consuming such a work of art with the artist, who went to great lengths to unearth the perfection. For some, it might come naturally. Or it might require deliberate work for everyone. Regardless, when you encounter such superlative works, it is hard not to be impressed. This is such a book.
