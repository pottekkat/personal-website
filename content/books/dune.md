Frank Herbert's masterpiece of science fiction, Dune tells the story of Paul Atreides, whose family accepts stewardship of the planet Arrakis, the only source of the most valuable substance in the universe—"the spice" melange. As dark forces explode into conflict over the planet's exclusive supply of spice, Paul is thrust into a future he never could have imagined.
