_**The Dictionary of Obscure Sorrows**_ is John Koenig's project, started in 2009, to put words to feelings that don't have names yet. I found it through [this Vsauce short](https://youtube.com/shorts/axILrr1GgMs), where Michael shares some of his favorites, like a word for a solitary experience you wish you could have shared with someone else.

Then he ends with this:

{{< blockquote author="John Koenig" link="https://youtube.com/shorts/axILrr1GgMs" title="The Dictionary of Obscure Sorrows" >}}
Aphemia, the fear that learning the name of something, a bird, a constellation, an attractive stranger, will somehow ruin it, inadvertently transforming a lucky discovery into a conceptual husk pinned in a glass case, leaving one less mystery fluttering around in the universe.
{{< /blockquote >}}
