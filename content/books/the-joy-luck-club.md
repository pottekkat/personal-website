_**The Joy Luck Club**_ is the second book set in San Francisco on my list, after [The Maltese Falcon](/books/the-maltese-falcon/). Amy Tan writes about four Chinese immigrant mothers and their American-born daughters, which seems like a good way to see the city as the melting pot it is.
