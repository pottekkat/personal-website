_**Hind Swaraj**_ is a short book Gandhi wrote in 1909 on a ship from London to South Africa. It came up in [episode 10 of Watching the Wheels](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LqoXRrhmAiw). The hosts discuss it critically, as evidence of the "really strange ideas" he held with a lot of certainty, calling railways, lawyers, and doctors evil. He apparently stood by it even in 1946.

I have to read it now to see for myself, as it is said to explain a lot about how Gandhi thought. Over the last few years, I have learned more about Gandhi's extraordinary life—especially the days after India's independence, which people often overlook—from the people behind [this exhibition](https://youicouldnotsavewalkwithme.org).
