{{< blockquote author="Below the Fray" link="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AlF3fWHIu_I" title="Why Writing Fiction is Worth It" >}}
I remember reading Malamud and these stories and feeling, oh, oh my god, across space and time, this was written just for me.
