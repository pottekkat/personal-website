_**Collected Fictions**_ is every short story Borges wrote, in one book. I came across it in [a review of Invisible Cities](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OFylXgPDTas), a book I [loved](/books/invisible-cities/). The reviewer says his first review on the channel was of Borges, and that it should be your desert island book:

{{< blockquote author="Better Than Food" link="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OFylXgPDTas" title="Italo Calvino - Invisible Cities BOOK REVIEW" >}}
If you just have one desert island book, one book for the rest of your life, that's the one.
{{< /blockquote >}}

Catherine K. also mentions Borges alongside Clarice Lispector in [this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N4eTH435w28) as two writers whose work feels like dreams.
