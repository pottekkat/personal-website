_**Groundwork of the Metaphysics of Morals**_ is my attempt at reading Immanuel Kant. Kant came up in [episode 10 of Watching the Wheels](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LqoXRrhmAiw), an episode on Gandhi, while discussing his insistence that the means matter, not just the ends. One of the hosts keeps coming back to Kant's categorical imperative: never treat people merely as a means to an end, but always as ends in themselves.

This is the short book where Kant lays out that idea.
