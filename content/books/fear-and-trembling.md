_**Fear and Trembling**_ came up while I was reading about angst and existentialism. Kierkegaard wrote it in 1843, and it is a good reminder that many feelings we think are unique to our generation have been around for centuries.

I also came across [this tweet](https://x.com/prateekwaghre/status/2089745756152602655) that led me to [a Mint piece on ghosting](https://www.livemint.com/mint-lounge/business-of-life/ghosting-mia-digital-life-umbrella-term-off-campus-11784352223148.html). TIL that Kierkegaard himself withdrew from his fiancée, Regine Olsen, for months before ending their engagement in a letter. He wrote this book two years after.
