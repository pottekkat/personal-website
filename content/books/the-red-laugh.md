_**The Red Laugh**_ is an anti-war novella by Leonid Andreyev, who is apparently called the Russian Edgar Allan Poe. I found it through [this review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8k4TP8Kdzq4) by Better Than Food, the same channel that pointed me to [Borges](/books/collected-fictions/).

{{< blockquote author="Better Than Food" link="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8k4TP8Kdzq4" title="The Red Laugh - Leonid Andreyev BOOK REVIEW" >}}
The Red Laugh is an anti-war novel... by way of hallucinatory psychological horror. This is one of the scariest things I have read in years.
{{< /blockquote >}}
