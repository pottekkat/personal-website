_**A Breath of Life**_ is Clarice Lispector's last book, published after her death. Lispector keeps coming up, including in [this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N4eTH435w28) by Catherine K., who reads her for the courage of perception:

{{< blockquote author="Catherine K." link="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N4eTH435w28" title="This book came to me in a dream" >}}
How far can I go into my thoughts? If I abandon all my fear... and I just trust in complete mystery, then what will I see?
{{< /blockquote >}}

I'm planning to start with [Near to the Wild Heart](/books/near-to-the-wild-heart/), her first, and end with this one, her last.
