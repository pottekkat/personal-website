_**Simple Passion**_ is my introduction to Annie Ernaux, through Henrik Karlsson's essay ["Good ideas"](https://www.henrikkarlsson.xyz/p/good-ideas). He writes about the techniques people use to get into and stay in the creative zone, and one of them is introducing a long delay between when you do the work and when the world sees it.

{{< blockquote author="Henrik Karlsson" link="https://www.henrikkarlsson.xyz/p/good-ideas" title="Good ideas" >}}
Annie Ernaux writes about this in A Simple Passion, a memoir about how she becomes obsessed in a banal way with a man who is having an affair with her—the thought that others will read these notes about the tacky sex life of a middle-aged woman feels, to her, almost fictional. She will be far away when it happens. Therefore, she doesn't feel a need to protect herself.
{{< /blockquote >}}

It is also very short, which makes it a good first book before moving on to _The Years_, which seems to be her best-known work.
