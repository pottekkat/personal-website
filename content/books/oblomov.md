_**Oblomov**_ is a book about decision paralysis and wasting your life. Ruby Hart brings it up in [her video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=615J7cYBZow) on national literary voices to show how Russian novels use flawed individuals to look at society:

{{< blockquote author="Ruby Hart" link="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=615J7cYBZow" title="How Every Country Has Its Own Literary Voice" >}}
This is a really comic story, but when you think about it, this is a very sad and... depressing story... this guy that just wants to absolutely waste his life away and just lay in bed forever.
{{< /blockquote >}}

Sounds a lot like [The Age of Reason](/books/the-age-of-reason/), another book on my list about betraying your own potential.
