_**Near to the Wild Heart**_ is Clarice Lispector's first novel, which she published in her early twenties. Lispector kept showing up everywhere I looked, including [this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vn-RR7_2R7A) about how people are moving from self-help to fiction. The creator walks into a new suburban Barnes & Noble and finds a whole shelf of Lispector.

I have a few of her books on my list, including [Água Viva](/books/agua-viva/), [The Passion According to G.H.](/books/the-passion-according-to-g-h/), and [A Breath of Life](/books/a-breath-of-life/). Starting with her first one seems right.
