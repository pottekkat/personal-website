_**Dreams**_ is a collection of Carl Jung's writings on dreams. I got it from Catherine K.'s video ["life is for reading."](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1SS7xrLDV-o), along with [Franny and Zooey](/books/franny-and-zooey/):

{{< blockquote author="Catherine K." link="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1SS7xrLDV-o" title="life is for reading." >}}
If you're a fan of dreams like I am, then you got to pick this book up. I think it's the best book out there that is overall a good book about dreams.
{{< /blockquote >}}

I'm reading a lot about existentialism lately, and Jung, who examines his own consciousness with such detachment, seems like a natural next step.
