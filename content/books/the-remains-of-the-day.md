_**The Remains of the Day**_ is one of the books I picked up from [this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=615J7cYBZow) by Ruby Hart on how each country has its own literary voice. She uses it twice: once as an example of Ishiguro's restraint and focus on memory, and again to show how class and institutions shape British characters.
