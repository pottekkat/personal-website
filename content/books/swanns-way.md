_**Swann's Way**_ is the first volume of Marcel Proust's _In Search of Lost Time_. Catherine K. includes it in her ["Fall thoughts, fall reads"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJ_Z4rOnjak) for its idea of involuntary memory, where a taste or a smell brings back a memory you thought you had forgotten.

{{< blockquote author="Catherine K." link="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJ_Z4rOnjak" title="Fall thoughts, fall reads" >}}
There's nothing more human than the idea of Proust.
{{< /blockquote >}}

The whole thing is seven volumes, so I'm committing to just this one for now.
