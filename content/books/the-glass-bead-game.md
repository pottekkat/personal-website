_**The Glass Bead Game**_ is Hesse's last novel. Ruby Hart uses it in [her video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=615J7cYBZow) as the main example of how German literature feels academic, with intellectual conflict taking the place of physical conflict.

{{< blockquote author="Ruby Hart" link="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=615J7cYBZow" title="How Every Country Has Its Own Literary Voice" >}}
You need to have all the kinds of intelligences... to be the master at this game... obviously this is a very intellectually and academically driven book.
{{< /blockquote >}}
