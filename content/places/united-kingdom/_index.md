London is the most expensive city I've ever been to. Even if you say {{< place "singapore" >}} is expensive to live in, you can easily get through the day staying cheap. But in London, there are no options.

Still, I really enjoyed the pace of the city. You really feel like you are among the things and part of something bigger while you are in the city. This is what I imagine New York would feel like.
