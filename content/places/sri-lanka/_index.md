I was still in school when I visited Sri Lanka. I remember it being very much like Kerala. The food was also quite similar. I remember the streets being nicer. In Kerala, we have narrow streets and virtually no place to walk. Srilanka looked like what Kerala could have been.

I will probably visit Sri Lanka again for a chill vacation.
