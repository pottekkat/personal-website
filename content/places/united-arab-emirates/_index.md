I had a long layover in Abu Dhabi, so I spent the day visiting the city.

It is a very indoor city, which makes sense given that you're in a desert.

I understand why people move to the UAE and think life is much better here. Especially the Malayalees.

But I do not like it. I prefer Kerala, and if I have to choose a city, I would prefer somewhere like Singapore or San Francisco.
